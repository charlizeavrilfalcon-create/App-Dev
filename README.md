# All Of Us Are Dead

## Genre
**Horror**, **Thriller**, **Action**, **Drama**, *Survival*

---

## Overview

> All of Us Are Dead is a South Korean zombie survival series released on Netflix. It follows high school students who become trapped in their school during a zombie outbreak.

---

## Basic Information

1. Original Title: 지금 우리 학교는
2. Release Date: January 28, 2022
3. Country: South Korea
4. Platform: Netflix
5. Episodes: 12
